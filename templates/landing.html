What is ClassEdge for?

One tool for the whole results season — from single spot-checks to full department batches.

🎯

Weighted, credit-unit accurate

Every course is graded on the standard 100-point breakdown, then weighted by its credit units: Quality Points = Units × Grade Point, and CGPA = ΣQP ÷ ΣCU. A 5-unit course counts five times as much as a 1-unit elective.

👥

One student or one hundred

Choose exactly how many students you're calculating for. The batch wizard walks you through the whole class and produces one formatted report for everyone.

📊

Class metrics & distribution

Highest and lowest averages, class mean GPA and a live A–F distribution chart — the department summary you used to build by hand in a spreadsheet.

🏆

Leaderboard & reveal moments

Results land in a sorted leaderboard, and every A-grade triggers a full-screen confetti reveal. Make results day fun again.

🔒

Hardened by design

Explicit ServeMux routing, body-size limits, method checks, security headers and full HTML escaping — a safe little server you can actually show your lecturer.

🖨️

Formatted, printable reports

The batch report renders in a clean monospace table you can copy, save or print — mirroring the classic department results sheet.

Worked example — why credit units matter

A student scores 85 in a 4-unit course and 65 in a 2-unit course.

  • 85 → A (5.0 GP) × 4 units = 20 QP
  • 65 → B (4.0 GP) × 2 units = 8 QP
  • CGPA = (20 + 8) ÷ (4 + 2) = 4.67 ✅
  • A flat average of grade points would say (5+4)÷2 = 4.50 — it ignores that the A came in a course worth double the units ❌
  • Cumulative across semesters: CGPA = Σ(all Quality Points) ÷ Σ(all Credit Units) — never the average of semester GPAs.
